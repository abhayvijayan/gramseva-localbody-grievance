# GramSeva – Local Body Grievance Management System

**GramSeva** is a digital grievance management system designed to improve communication between citizens and local government bodies. It aims to simplify complaint submission, enable grievance tracking, and help local authorities manage and resolve public issues more efficiently.

## 📌 About the Project

Citizens often face difficulties reporting local issues and tracking the progress of their complaints. GramSeva aims to address these challenges by providing a centralized digital platform for managing grievances related to local body services.

The system is designed to make grievance handling more transparent, organized, and accessible while improving coordination between citizens and the responsible authorities.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Digitize the grievance submission and management process.
- Make it easier for citizens to report local issues.
- Improve transparency in complaint handling.
- Enable systematic tracking of grievance status.
- Help local authorities organize and manage reported issues.
- Encourage accountability and efficient public service delivery.

## ✨ Key Features

The project is intended to support the following functionality:

- **Grievance Submission:** A platform for citizens to report local issues and submit complaints.
- **Grievance Tracking:** Track the progress and status of submitted grievances.
- **Complaint Management:** Organize and manage grievances reported by citizens.
- **Status Updates:** Communicate changes in grievance status.
- **Authority Dashboard:** Provide local body officials with a centralized interface for handling complaints.
- **Transparent Workflow:** Improve visibility into the grievance handling process.

*Note: The features listed above describe the project's intended functionality; their implementation may vary.*

## 🏛️ Problem Statement

Traditional grievance handling processes can involve manual paperwork, limited communication, and difficulties tracking the progress of complaints. These challenges may delay resolution and make it harder for citizens to understand the actions taken on their reports.

GramSeva explores how software can help modernize this process by connecting citizens and local authorities through a centralized grievance management platform.

## 💡 Proposed Workflow

1. A citizen submits a grievance through the platform.
2. The grievance is recorded in the system.
3. The responsible authority reviews the complaint.
4. The grievance status is updated as it progresses.
5. The citizen can check the status of the complaint.
6. The grievance is marked as resolved when the issue is addressed.
